Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding campus placements.

Any registered university student with an active profile can explore and apply to open recruitment drives. Ensure you meet the minimum CGPA and branch requirements specified on each drive's details page.

You can upload your profile picture directly from the Login page, Registration page, or by navigating to My Profile in the top navigation bar. You can also upload or replace your PDF resume at any time.

Company recruiters can log in to their Recruiter Dashboard to see all published drives. Clicking Review Applicants displays all candidate profiles, CGPAs, resumes, and allows 1-click status updating (Shortlist, Select, Reject).

The portal features dual database support: it connects to MySQL if a local MySQL instance is running, and automatically falls back to SQLite (campus_recruitment.db) with zero manual setup required.
